## What we build

UniqueApps focuses on small, practical products that give people more control over their devices and data.

- **Offline connectivity** using Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth
- **Private communication and sharing** without unnecessary internet dependence
- **Android utilities** designed with safety and clarity in mind
- **Simple interfaces** that feel useful from the first launch

## Featured projects

| Project | What it does | Repository |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **DPI Switcher** | Change Android SmallestWidth/DPI without reboot, with presets, Quick Tile, Root, and Shizuku support. | [Open repository](https://github.com/uniqueappsorg/DPI-SWITCHER) |
| **UniqueChat** | Private offline messaging powered by Wi-Fi Direct. | [Open repository](https://github.com/uniqueappsorg/UniqueChat) |
| **UniqueShare** | Fast local file sharing over Wi-Fi Direct. | [Open repository](https://github.com/uniqueappsorg/UniqueShare) |
| **You Are Mine** | Close-range messaging built around Bluetooth Low Energy. | [Open repository](https://github.com/uniqueappsorg/YouAreMine) |
| **UniqueSync** | Synchronized music playback across nearby devices. | [Open repository](https://github.com/uniqueappsorg/UniqueSync) |
